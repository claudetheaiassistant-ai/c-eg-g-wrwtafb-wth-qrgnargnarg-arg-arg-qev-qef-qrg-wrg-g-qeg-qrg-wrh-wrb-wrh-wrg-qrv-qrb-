Settings

Without a key, Jarvis searches your notes locally. To use AI, add a Google Gemini API key.

Saved only in this browser on this device if you tap Save. It is sent directly to Google when you ask a question. Anyone with access to this browser could retrieve it; don't use a shared device.

Back up your notes

Safari data can be cleared. Export a backup to Files; import it here to restore or move your notes.

Backups contain your notes but never your API key. Import replaces the notes on this device.

Voice

Start voice conversation in Chat and allow microphone access. Jarvis listens, then speaks its reply aloud. Tap Stop voice to stop listening and speaking. Voice recognition requires browser support and may use a speech service; it may not be private or work offline. If unsupported, type your message instead.

About

Jarvis Phone is a separate, lightweight edition. It does not automatically sync with Jarvis OG. Screen monitoring and desktop focus controls are not included.

Google's free tier may have quotas or regional restrictions. Check your Google AI Studio project and pricing; the app cannot guarantee free API usage.
